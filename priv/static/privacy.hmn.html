Peb sau dab tsi, thiab vim li cas

Thaum koj nkag nrog Google, peb tsuas txais thiab khaws:

  • ib tus naj npawb account los ntawm Google, kom peb paub tias yog koj zaum tom ntej;
  • koj lub npe hu — peb tsis khaws koj lub npe puv;
  • ib daim duab me me theej los ntawm koj daim duab profile, yog koj muaj — ua thaum koj nkag, hloov tshiab txhua zaus koj nkag, thiab rho tawm nrog koj tus account.

Peb tsis thov los yog khaws koj tus email, koj cov neeg sib cuag, los yog lwm yam dab tsi los ntawm koj tus account Google. Koj lub npe thiab duab tsuas los ntawm tus account ntawd xwb; yog xav hloov, hloov rau ntawm Google, ces lawv yuav hloov ntawm no zaum tom ntej koj nkag, los yog thaum koj nias Hloov kho los ntawm Google (nias koj daim duab sab saud laug, ces Hloov).

Thaum koj siv lub app:

  • Koj thaj chaw kwv yees — thaum koj npaj tsav tsheb los yog thov caij tsheb, daim ntawv qhia chaw qhia koj nyob hauv ib thaj chaw dav li 350 m (kwv yees li ib feem plaub mais), tsis qhia koj qhov chaw tseeb li. Peb siv nws los qhia koj rau cov neeg nyob ze.
  • Koj qhov chaw nyob tseeb thaum caij tsheb — tsuas qhia rau koj tus phooj ywg caij tsheb xwb, tsuas yog thaum caij tsheb xwb, kom neb nrhiav tau neb.
  • Cov lus koj xa rau lwm tus, thiab kev thov caij tsheb.
  • Koj tus IP address, ib pliag xwb, los tiv thaiv kev siv tsis zoo (piv txwv, ib tug neeg xa ntau heev kom lub kev pab cuam puas).
  • Cov ntaub ntawv teeb meem technical — sau tseg cov yuam kev thiab cov lus ceeb toom, los kho teeb meem. Lawv tsis muaj koj lub npe, koj qhov chaw nyob, los yog koj cov lus; ib qho yuam kev yuav hais txog ib tus naj npawb account.

Ntawm koj lub cuab yeej: ib lub cookie nkag ua rau koj nyob hauv (txog thaum koj tawm, lossis 30 hnub tom qab koj siv lub app zaum kawg; tsis pub dhau ib xyoos), thiab lub app nco qab thaj chaw ntawm daim ntawv qhia chaw uas koj siv zaum kawg kom daim ntawv qhia chaw qhib rau qhov koj nyob, thiab cov lus twg koj twb nyeem lawm kom cov tshiab qhia tias tsis tau nyeem (tsuas yog cov twg xwb, tsis yog cov lus nws tus kheej). Thaum koj tawm, lawv raug rho tawm. Yog koj xaiv ib hom lus, ib lub cookie nco qab koj qhov kev xaiv rau ib xyoos (thaum tawm nws tseem nyob, kom nplooj nkag tseem yog koj hom lus); yog koj tsis xaiv, lub app siv koj lub xov tooj hom lus thiab tsis khaws dab tsi. Cov no yuav tsum muaj kom lub app ua haujlwm; peb tsis siv cookies tshaj tawm muag khoom los yog taug qab.

Cov kev pab cuam peb tso siab rau

  • Cloudflare nqa tag nrho cov kev txuas mus rau Hood Ride thiab tiv thaiv nws ntawm kev tawm tsam; nws tswj kev txuas kom muaj kev nyab xeeb, yog li nws pom tau cov kev txuas uas hla dhau nws. Nws kuj suav cov neeg tuaj saib rau peb (Cloudflare Web Analytics): nplooj twg raug qhib, hom cuab yeej thiab browser twg, lub vev xaib twg txuas los ntawm no, thiab cov nplooj thauj ceev npaum li cas. Nws tsis siv cookies, tsis taug koj qab mus rau lwm lub vev xaib, thiab peb tsuas pom tus lej tag nrho xwb.
  • Google muab kev nkag. Thaum koj nkag, Hood Ride theej koj daim duab profile cia rau nws tus kheej; ces cov duab raug qhia los ntawm Hood Ride lub server, yog li Google tsis pom leej twg saib leej twg daim duab. Yog koj nias Qhia kev thaum caij tsheb, Google Maps yuav qhib nrog qhov chaw koj yuav mus.

Tsis Txhob Taug Kuv Qab (Do Not Track)

Qee lub browser xa ib lub teeb liab "Tsis Txhob Taug Kuv Qab" (Do Not Track). Hood Ride tsis taug koj qab hla cov vev xaib thiab tsis cia lwm tus taug koj qab ntawm Hood Ride, yog li lub teeb liab tsis hloov dab tsi ntawm no — peb twb ua raws li nws thov lawm.

Kev nyab xeeb

Tag nrho cov kev txuas raug kaw zais (HTTPS). Peb khaws tsawg li tsawg tau, thiab feem ntau ntawm yam lub app siv tsuas nyob hauv lub server lub cim xeeb xwb thiab ploj sai tom qab koj mus offline.

Kev hloov txoj cai no

Yog peb hloov txoj cai no, peb yuav hloov kho nplooj no thiab nws hnub pib siv.
