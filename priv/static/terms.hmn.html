Qhov no yog ib qho kev txhais lus kom yooj yim rau koj. Yog tias nws txawv ntawm tsab lus Askiv, tsab lus Askiv yog tsab siv, tshwj tsis yog txoj cai lij choj ntawm qhov chaw koj nyob hais lwm yam. Nyeem tsab lus Askiv

Thaum koj siv Hood Ride, koj pom zoo rau cov cai no thiab rau peb Txoj Cai Tswj Ntiag Tug. Yog koj tsis pom zoo, tsis txhob siv.

1. Hood Ride yog dab tsi

Hood Ride pab cov neeg nyob ze sib nrhiav kom caij tsheb ua ke, thiab tsis muaj dab tsi ntxiv. Peb tsis yog ib lub tuam txhab tsheb taxi, tsheb thauj neeg los yog hu tsheb. Peb tsis muab kev caij tsheb, tsis ntiav tus tsav tsheb, thiab tsis koom nrog ib qho kev caij tsheb twg. Txhua qhov kev caij tsheb yog ib qho kev npaj ntawm tus caij tsheb thiab tus tsav tsheb nkaus xwb; Hood Ride tsis yog ib tog ntawm nws.

2. Tsis muaj kev kuaj xyuas

Peb tsis kuaj xyuas leej twg li: tsis kuaj keeb kwm, tsis kuaj daim ntawv tsav tsheb, kev pov hwm los yog tsheb, thiab tsis kuaj leej twg yog leej twg dhau ntawm koj tus account nkag. Cov npe thiab duab los ntawm tus account ntawd thiab tej zaum yuav tsis yog tiag. Siv koj tus kheej txoj kev txiav txim siab txog leej twg koj caij nrog.

3. Leej twg siv tau

Koj yuav tsum muaj 18 xyoo los yog laus dua. Ib tug neeg ib tus account.

4. Kev them nyiaj

Hood Ride yeej tsis tswj, teeb los yog txais ib qho kev them nyiaj twg. Txhua qhov kev npaj ntawm ib tus caij tsheb thiab ib tus tsav tsheb yog nyob ntawm lawv nkaus xwb, thiab lawv yog tus ris lub txim.

5. Tsis muaj kev pov hwm

Hood Ride tsis muab ib hom kev pov hwm twg li, rau tus tsav tsheb, tus caij tsheb, tsheb los yog khoom. Cov tsav tsheb yog tus saib xyuas lawv tus kheej kev pov hwm.

6. Koj lub luag haujlwm

  • Cov tsav tsheb: yuav tsum muaj daim ntawv tsav tsheb siv tau, daim ntawv sau npe tsheb thiab kev pov hwm, thiab ib lub tsheb muaj kev nyab xeeb, thiab ua raws tag nrho cov cai tsav tsheb.
  • Txhua tus: koj yog tus saib xyuas kev ua raws li txoj cai lij choj ntawm qhov chaw koj caij, thiab koj tus kheej kev nyab xeeb thiab tus cwj pwm.

7. Ua neeg zoo

Tsis txhob:

  • thab, hem, raws qab los yog ntxub ntxaug leej twg;
  • siv Hood Ride ua dab tsi txhaum cai;
  • tsim cov account cuav los yog ob tus, los yog ua txuj ua lwm tus neeg;
  • xa spam los yog kev tshaj tawm muag khoom;
  • siv bots los yog scripts, los yog sau lwm tus neeg cov ntaub ntawv;
  • sim ua kom puas, ua kom hnyav dhau, theej los yog hla lub app los yog nws cov kev tiv thaiv.

8. Kev nyab xeeb thiab xwm txheej ceev

Peb tsis tuaj yeem lav tias leej twg yog leej twg, lawv lub siab xav li cas los yog lawv tsav tsheb li cas. Yog muaj dab tsi zoo li tsis yog, tsis txhob caij. Thaum muaj xwm txheej ceev, hu rau koj cheeb tsam tus xov tooj xwm txheej ceev (911 hauv Tebchaws Meskas). Hood Ride tsis yog kev pab cuam xwm txheej ceev, thiab cov chaw ntawm daim ntawv qhia chaw yog kwv yees xwb: tsis txhob tso siab rau lawv thaum muaj xwm txheej ceev.

9. Koj tus account

Koj yog tus saib xyuas koj tus account thiab txhua yam uas ua nrog nws. Khaws koj tus account nkag kom muaj kev nyab xeeb, tsis txhob cia lwm tus siv, thiab qhia rau peb tam sim ntawd yog muaj leej twg siv.

10. Yam koj qhia

Lwm cov neeg siv pom qee yam koj qhia, xws li koj lub npe, duab thiab qhov chaw nyob kwv yees; peb Txoj Cai Tswj Ntiag Tug piav meej meej tias leej twg pom dab tsi. Peb tsis yog tus saib xyuas yam lwm cov neeg siv ua nrog ib yam dab tsi koj qhia rau lawv.

11. Kev rho cov account tawm

Peb yuav ncua los yog rho ib tus account twg tawm txhua lub sijhawm, muaj los yog tsis muaj kev ceeb toom, vim ua txhaum cov cai no los yog vim ib yam dab tsi uas peb ntseeg tias ua rau tib neeg muaj kev txaus ntshai.

12. Tsis muaj kev lav

Hood Ride yog dawb thiab muab "raws li nws yog" thiab "raws li muaj", tsis muaj ib hom kev lav twg li. Tej zaum nws yuav siv tsis tau, qeeb los yog tsis raug, thiab tej zaum nws yuav ploj cov lus los yog cov ntaub ntawv. Peb tsis cog lus tias yuav nrhiav tau kev caij tsheb, los yog tias leej twg yog tus lawv hais tias lawv yog.

13. Peb lub luag haujlwm txwv

Raws li txoj cai lij choj tso cai tau ntau tshaj plaws: (a) Hood Ride thiab tus khiav nws tsis yog tus saib xyuas ib yam dab tsi uas tshwm sim rau, thaum, los yog vim ib qho kev caij tsheb los yog kev sib ntsib uas npaj los ntawm lub app, suav nrog kev raug mob, kev tuag, kev puas tsuaj rau khoom, kev poob, kev raug nyiag, los yog tus cwj pwm ntawm ib tus neeg siv twg; (b) peb tsis ris lub txim rau ib qho kev puas tsuaj tsis ncaj qha, kev puas tsuaj tshwm sim nrog, kev puas tsuaj tshwj xeeb, kev puas tsuaj uas tshwm sim tom qab, los yog kev puas tsuaj rau txim (indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages); thiab (c) peb lub luag haujlwm tag nrho rau koj rau lwm qhov kev thov twg yog txwv rau $100. Qee qhov chaw tsis tso cai rau cov kev txwv no, yog li lawv tsuas siv raws li txoj cai lij choj tso cai xwb.

14. Koj ris koj tus kheej tej kev ua

Yog koj kev siv Hood Ride, koj kev caij tsheb, koj tus cwj pwm, los yog koj kev ua txhaum cov cai no ua rau muaj ib qho kev thov tawm tsam Hood Ride los yog tus khiav nws, koj pom zoo them cov nqi, suav nrog cov nqi kws lij choj uas tsim nyog.

15. Lub sijhawm txwv rau kev thov

Txhua qhov kev thov ntawm koj thiab Hood Ride yuav tsum coj los hauv lub sijhawm uas txoj cai lij choj siv tau tso cai.

16. Peb li, thiab koj tej tswv yim

Hood Ride, nws lub npe thiab nws software yog peb li; cov cai no tsis muab ib txoj cai twg rau koj rau lawv dhau ntawm kev siv lub app. Yog koj xa tswv yim los yog lus tawm tswv yim rau peb, peb siv tau lawv ywj pheej, tsis tshuav koj dab tsi.

17. Cov ntsiab lus me

  • Kev sib cais: yog ib feem twg ntawm cov cai no siv tsis tau, cov seem tseem siv tau.
  • Tsis tso tseg txoj cai: yog ib zaug peb tsis yuam ib txoj cai, peb tsis tau tso tseg txoj cai yuam nws.
  • Tag nrho daim ntawv cog lus: cov cai no thiab Txoj Cai Tswj Ntiag Tug yog tag nrho daim ntawv cog lus ntawm koj thiab Hood Ride.
  • Dhau peb lub peev xwm tswj: peb tsis yog tus saib xyuas tej kev ua tsis tiav uas tshwm sim los ntawm tej yam dhau peb lub peev xwm tswj uas tsim nyog, xws li kev tu ncua, kev puas tsuaj loj, los yog kev ua tsis tiav ntawm cov kev pab cuam peb tso siab rau.
  • Kev hloov mus: peb yuav hloov cov cai no mus rau ib tus uas los hloov peb, xws li ib lub tuam txhab uas los tuav Hood Ride.

18. Kev hloov

Peb yuav hloov kho cov cai no. Peb yuav hloov hnub saum toj no, thiab rau kev hloov tseem ceeb peb yuav thov kom koj rov pom zoo dua hauv lub app.
